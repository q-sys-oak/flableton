# Barline

**Make music anywhere.** Load a beat, punch in your vocals, auto-tune them, mix, and export. It runs in Chrome on a computer, or in your phone's browser.

**Open the app:** https://barline-app.pages.dev

You can also download `Barline.html` from this repo and double-click it to run offline in Chrome.

Quick start:

1. Tap **🎵 Load beat** and pick an MP3 or WAV. The tempo is detected for you.
2. Go to **🎙 Record**, tap **Allow mic**, then **+ Lead**.
3. Hit **●** to record. Stop and it jumps back, so you can punch in the next take or tap **Redo last take**.

This is a beta. Send feedback to the Barline team.
